Where it comes from

AZ Professional Partners started in January 2011 as a Tucson referral group built on one idea: reputation is one of the most precious assets a business has, so every member had to be someone another member could vouch for. The goal was, and is, to help local professionals grow through business relationships.

How joining works

1Tell us about your business, with the link to its Google listing.
2We check the listing is a real Tucson business. Usually within two business days.
3Your invite link arrives by email. Join, say hello, and introduce your business.

We use what you send only to check your business and welcome you. We never sell it or add you to a mailing list.
