Questions

Good things to ask before you pick

Is this just a website?

Yes, in the way a town square is just pavement. It's a website underneath, found on Google like any other. What changes is what it's for: a place people come back to, not a flyer they read once.

Why is the Free Site free?

You pay for it in advertising: a slim line at the top of every page that says AZ Tech built it. We build it from one or two conversations, you own your domain, and we host it at no cost to you. At the end you get an invoice showing what the site is worth, $800, with the full amount waived.

What's the difference between Free and Starter?

Only where our credit sits. Free has a slim bar across the top of every page; Starter moves it to a small line in your footer, so your header is all yours. Same site, same free hosting, and changes are paid the same way.

Can I sell things on my site?

Not on the Free Site. Free hosting isn't allowed to run a store. If you need a shop or a database, we build it on the right platform for it, like Shopify, or on our own servers.

What is AZ Professional Partners?

Our Slack for Tucson business owners: a place to talk to each other about questions, referrals and what's working. Every Free Site comes with an invite, and any Tucson business can request one here.

What's the difference between Own-It and Team?

Own-It is for one person who wants to make their own changes, and there's no monthly bill. Team is for several people editing a live site that has to be watched, so it comes with monthly monitoring and maintenance.

Who owns my domain and my content?

You do, on every door. If you ever leave, your name and your words go with you.

Does the $75 a month include changes?

No. It keeps the site safe, updated and running. Changes are separate: pay for each one, or add AI Edits for unlimited changes. We email you the current prices.
